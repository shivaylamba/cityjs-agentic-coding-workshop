## Design Context

### Users
Frontend developers participating in a live three-hour CityJS coding workshop; instructor needs a clear live delivery sequence. Attendees are overwhelmed by long prose.

### Brand Personality
Direct, calm, practical.

### Aesthetic Direction
Preserve the warm-paper and teal design. Short task-first lab pages; avoid textbook-length default content.

### Design Principles
- One short topic explanation.
- Exactly three numbered actions per live lab.
- Two observable finish checks.
- Prompt help and notes collapsed by default.
- Instructor scripts remain available separately.
